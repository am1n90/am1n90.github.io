Creator Engine

Creator Engine is a personal Windows desktop application used by its author to produce and publish YouTube Shorts for two channels the author owns: Wow Daily (@wowdaily_tv) and Lolo's World (@lolosworld_tv).

This is not a sign-in page and not a public service. There is no website account, no registration and no hosted backend. The application runs on the author's own computer.

What the application does

  • Helps choose a topic and write a script, with every fact tied to a source.
  • Generates a voice-over, visuals and captions, and assembles the video locally.
  • Shows a preview, then uploads the video to the author's own YouTube channel as private. Making a video public always requires a separate click by the author.
  • Reads the channel's own video statistics so the author can see how published videos perform.

YouTube access

The application connects to YouTube through Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs, only for channels owned by the author. Exactly what is accessed and how it is stored is described in the Privacy Policy.
