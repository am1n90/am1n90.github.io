Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Creator Engine ("the application") is a personal desktop application for Windows that its author uses to produce and publish YouTube Shorts on the author's own channels. The application runs on the author's computer. It has no server operated by the author, and the author does not receive, collect or store any data from the application on any server.

1. Google user data the application accesses

When the author connects a YouTube channel, the application requests these OAuth scopes:

ScopeWhat the application uses it for
youtube.uploadUpload a finished video to the connected channel, always with privacy status private.
youtube.readonlyRead the connected channel's own details (title, handle, thumbnail, uploads list) and the status and statistics of its videos; search public YouTube videos to study trends for topic ideas.
youtube.force-sslChange the privacy status of a video the application uploaded (private, scheduled or public) only after the author clicks Publish or Schedule; upload caption tracks and translated titles and descriptions for the author's own video; read the video's status back to confirm the result.
yt-analytics.readonlyRead the connected channel's own analytics (views and subscribers gained per video) to show results in the application.

The application does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service, and it does not read comments.

2. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features visible in the application: connecting the author's channels, uploading and publishing the author's own videos, and showing the author's own channel and video statistics. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, rented or transferred to third parties, and is not used to build profiles, to train machine-learning models or for any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. Where the data is stored

  • OAuth tokens. The refresh token for each connected channel, and the author's own Google OAuth client credentials, are stored in the Windows Credential Manager on the author's computer. They are not written to files, logs or environment variables.
  • Channel and video statistics. Data read from YouTube (such as the channel title and handle, video statuses, view counts and subscribers gained) is kept in a local database in the application's data folder on the author's computer, together with the videos and projects the application creates.

Nothing from these locations is sent to any server operated by the author.

4. What leaves the computer

  • Google and YouTube. Requests to Google's OAuth and YouTube APIs, and uploads of the author's own videos, captions and metadata to the author's channel.
  • Anthropic API (Claude). To propose topics, write scripts and check facts, the application sends Anthropic: the channel's style and content rules written by the author; the topic the author typed; and, for topic suggestions, the public title, view count and ID of popular public videos found by searching YouTube. Claude's web search feature may also search the open web for sources. Not sent to Anthropic: OAuth tokens, the author's Google account details, the author's channel analytics or subscriber numbers, or other private data from the author's YouTube account. Anthropic processes this content under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Optional video-generation services. If the author turns on a cloud video generator (for example Kling or Higgsfield), the text description of a scene and the author's credential for that service are sent to it. No YouTube account data is sent. Without such a service, visuals are generated on the author's computer.
  • Model downloads. The application may download open-source speech models from public sources such as GitHub. No personal data is sent.

The application contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the author's computer until the author deletes it. Disconnecting a channel in the application removes its stored token from the Windows Credential Manager. Uninstalling the application and deleting its data folder removes the local database.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by selecting Creator Engine and choosing to delete all connections. You can also disconnect the channel inside the application.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Creator Engine's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The application also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The application is a tool for its adult author and is not directed to children. It collects no personal data from anyone other than the author. Some of the author's channels publish content aimed at children; such videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube as required.
